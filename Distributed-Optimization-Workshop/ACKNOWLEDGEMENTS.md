# Acknowledgements and Educational References

This workshop was developed as a Persian-language educational resource connecting distributed optimization theory with practical numerical modeling and implementation.

## CVXPY

We gratefully acknowledge the CVXPY developers and the official CVXPY documentation and example collection.

- `Linear Programming.ipynb` includes a reference to Winston and Goldberg, *Operations Research: Applications and Algorithms*.
- `Nonlinear Programming.ipynb` states that its box-volume example is adapted from Boyd, Kim, Vandenberghe, and Hassibi, *A Tutorial on Geometric Programming*.

## CasADi
